1. The service

MemoPic helps you understand everyday German letters and learn practical German. You can photograph a letter or enter text, receive an explanation in your chosen language (what the letter is, who sent it, what you may need to do and possible deadlines), key German phrases and a draft reply. The App also offers deadline reminders, spaced-repetition review of phrases, AI role-play speaking practice and a daily goal.

We may develop, change or discontinue features. Some features are only available with a MemoPic Pro subscription.

2. AI explanations can be wrong

Explanations, translations, deadlines, phrases and reply drafts are generated automatically by artificial intelligence. They can be incomplete, outdated or wrong — for example a date or amount may be misread.

Always check the original letter, in particular names, amounts, dates and deadlines. Deadlines in German letters often depend on the date of delivery, which the App cannot know. A reminder in the App does not replace your own check of the deadline. You are responsible for any reply you send; check drafts before using them.
