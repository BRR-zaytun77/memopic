Questions

Good to know

Which letters does Klarineo understand? +
Letters from the Jobcenter, Finanzamt, Ausländerbehörde, Krankenkasse, Rundfunkbeitrag, insurers and more — including reminders (Mahnung) and official notices. If it came in a German envelope, Klarineo is built to help you make sense of it.
Do I need to speak German? +
No. Klarineo explains the letter in your own language and can draft the German reply for you to review. It works in English, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic and Uzbek.
Is my information kept private? +
Yes. Klarineo is GDPR-compliant, processes data on EU infrastructure, and does not use your letters to train AI models. There are no ads and no ad tracking. See our Privacy Policy for the details.
Is Klarineo legal advice? +
No. Klarineo helps you understand your mail and prepare a response, but it is not a lawyer and does not provide legal advice. For binding decisions, consult a qualified professional.
