Bußgeldstelle

Bußgeldbescheid and Anhörung: German traffic fine letters explained

Last reviewed: 25 October 20263 min read
In short

Traffic offences in Germany are handled in steps. For small offences you may get a warning fine (Verwarnungsgeld). For others you first receive a hearing form (Anhörungsbogen) or a witness form (Zeugenfragebogen), then possibly a formal fine notice (Bußgeldbescheid). Against a Bußgeldbescheid you can file an Einspruch within two weeks after delivery.

Who sends these letters?

Traffic fines are issued by the fine office (Bußgeldstelle) of the city, district or police authority where the offence happened — for example for speeding, parking or running a red light. The letter is addressed to the driver if known, otherwise usually to the registered keeper of the vehicle (Halter).

The different letters

  • Verwarnung mit Verwarnungsgeld: a warning with a small fine for minor offences. If you pay within the stated period, the matter is usually closed. If you do not pay, the authority can start formal fine proceedings.
  • Anhörungsbogen: a hearing form. You are accused of an offence and may comment. You must give correct personal details (Angaben zur Person), but you do not have to comment on the accusation itself.
  • Zeugenfragebogen: a witness form sent to the vehicle keeper if someone else may have been driving. Close relatives may have the right to refuse to testify (Zeugnisverweigerungsrecht) — the form usually explains this.
  • Bußgeldbescheid: the formal fine notice. It states the offence, the fine, fees and expenses, and possibly points or a driving ban. It is officially delivered, often in a yellow envelope with the delivery date noted.

The two-week deadline

Against a Bußgeldbescheid, you can file an Einspruch (objection) within two weeks after delivery (Zustellung) — § 67 of the Administrative Offences Act (OWiG). The objection must be made in writing or recorded at the authority. If you do not object, the notice becomes final and you have to pay. Write down the delivery date from the envelope.

Higher fines can also bring points in the driving aptitude register in Flensburg (Fahreignungsregister), kept by the Federal Motor Transport Authority (Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt). Serious offences can lead to a driving ban (Fahrverbot).

Beware of fake fines

Be cautious with emails or text messages that ask you to pay a traffic fine via a link. Official German fine letters normally arrive by post and name the authority, a file number (Aktenzeichen) and bank details of a public authority. If in doubt, contact the authority using contact details you found yourself, not those in the message.

What to do

  1. Identify the type of letterWarning, hearing form, witness form or Bußgeldbescheid — the next step depends on it.
  2. Note the dateFor a Bußgeldbescheid, write down the delivery date from the yellow envelope. The two-week period starts then.
  3. Check the detailsDate, time, place, vehicle and photo. Were you really the driver?
  4. Decide: pay or objectIf everything is correct, pay by the date stated. If not, file an Einspruch in time, ideally after advice.
  5. Get advice for points or a driving banA lawyer specialising in traffic law can check the case. Ask whether you have legal expenses insurance.

Useful German phrases

  • Aktenzeichen: …File number: …
  • Hiermit lege ich Einspruch gegen den Bußgeldbescheid vom … ein.I hereby object to the fine notice dated ….
  • Ich bitte um Akteneinsicht.I request access to the file.
  • Zur Sache mache ich keine Angaben.I will not comment on the matter.
  • Ich habe das Fahrzeug zu diesem Zeitpunkt nicht gefahren.I was not driving the vehicle at that time.

Where to get free or low-cost help

  • Beratungshilfe (legal advice aid)If your income is low, you can apply at your local Amtsgericht (district court) for a Beratungshilfeschein. With it, a lawyer can advise you for a small fixed fee.
  • Verbraucherzentrale (consumer advice centre)Consumer advice centres exist in every federal state. They help with bills, contracts, payment reminders and Inkasso letters. Some information is free; individual advice may cost a small fee.

Official sources

  • Administrative Offences Act (OWiG), § 67 — form and deadline of the objection
  • Federal Motor Transport Authority (kba.de) — driving aptitude register
