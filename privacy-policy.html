Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Klarineo (“we”, “us”) processes when you use the Klarineo mobile application, why we process it, and the choices and rights you have. We built Klarineo to help you understand official German letters — and to do so while respecting your privacy.

1. Our privacy principles

  • We process the data we need to provide the service, and not more.
  • We do not show ads and we do not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not use the content of your letters to train AI models.
  • We process data on infrastructure located in the European Union.

2. Information we process

To provide and improve the service, we process the following:

  • Letters you scan: images or PDFs you submit and the text extracted from them. This content is processed to generate the explanation, extract the deadline, prepare a reply draft, and provide related features you request.
  • Language and app preferences: such as your chosen interface language, so the app works the way you expect.
  • Device & technical information: basic information such as device model and operating system version, used to keep the app stable and secure.
  • Usage information: limited, privacy-respecting information about how features are used, to help us improve the app. This is not used for advertising.

You decide what you scan. Please avoid submitting documents that contain information you do not wish to process.

3. How we use your information

  • To explain your letter in your language and extract the relevant deadline (Frist).
  • To prepare reply drafts, reminders, and speaking-practice sessions you request.
  • To warn you about potentially fraudulent or suspicious mail (scam shield).
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the service.

4. AI processing and service providers

Generating explanations and drafts involves automated (AI) processing of the text from your letter. To do this we may use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf as processors under data-processing agreements and applicable EU data-protection law.

  • Processing takes place on infrastructure located in the European Union.
  • The content of your letters is not used to train AI models.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed to provide the service and for the purposes described in this policy, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Where the app stores your documents and history on your device, you can remove them from within the app.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”);
  • request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to limit access to it.

8. Children's privacy

Klarineo is intended for adults dealing with official correspondence and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Not legal advice

Klarineo helps you understand your mail and prepare responses. It does not provide legal advice and is not a substitute for a qualified professional. For binding decisions, please consult a lawyer or the relevant authority.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
